## Licensing: independently re-verified, not just re-stated

This delivery's own audit documents (`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`, `specs/level-data-license-memo.md`, `docs/executable-parity-report.md`) are unusually thorough — every specific, checkable claim in them was independently re-verified against the primary source before being accepted, rather than summarized on trust:

- Extracted `reference/os4depot/bubbletrain-os4-1.0final.lha` (349 files, matching the documented archive entry count) with 7-Zip and confirmed **93 of 94** `.cpp`/`.h` files carry the exact upstream header "Copyright (C) 2004 Adam Child... Craig Marshall... GNU General Public License... version 2... or (at your option) any later version" — the one exception being `src/List.h`, confirmed by reading it directly to be Ron Penton's `DLinkedList.h` with no license grant at all.
- Confirmed the root `README`'s statement "Bubble Train is released under the GPL license" is real and verbatim (SHA-256 of the file matches the memo's cited hash exactly), including its unfinished `[[[[[[[[[[[[[LINK IN HERE]]]]]]]]]]]` placeholder — an unmistakable sign of an authentic, unedited historical file rather than a paraphrase.
- Confirmed the historical `doc/help.html` documents `.gms`/`.lvl` as the game's own editable game/level definition format (its SHA-256 also matches the memo's cited hash exactly), supporting the memo's conclusion that these XML files are "source" in the FSF sense, not incidental media.
- Byte-compared all **61 `.lvl` + 5 `.gms`** files inside the OS4 archive against the delivered `public/data/original-levels/files/` copies: all 66 matched exactly.
- Independently re-read the embedded metadata of `click.wav`, `cannon_move.wav`, and `cannon_fire.wav`, and located the `font-agentorange`, `font_arial`, and `font_bubbleboy` filenames under `themes/`, confirming every specific third-party-media claim in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.
- Verified the three archives listed in `reference/manifest.json` (`bubbletrain-os4-1.0final.lha`, `bubbletrain-os4-earlier-as-received.lha`, `BubbleTrain_GP2X-2006_Entry.zip`) hash-match exactly.
- Re-ran this project's own `tools/release-check.mjs` (hash/quarantine audit), `tools/release-soak.mjs` (128,589-tick simulation across all 61 levels) and `tools/http-smoke.mjs` (HTTP/MIME delivery) after the `public/` restructuring — all pass unchanged.
- Spot-checked the executable-parity claims in `docs/executable-parity-report.md`: independently re-hashed both historical binaries (OS4 PowerPC `bubbletrain`, GP2X ARM `BubTrain`) against the report's cited SHA-256 values — both matched exactly — and confirmed the GP2X binary's retained debug strings include `rippleMove`, `triggerBomb`, `rotateLeft`, `calcRadius` and 38 occurrences of `TrackSpiral`, corroborating that the named functions are real, locatable symbols rather than an invented narrative.

## What was not ported, and why

- **`src/List.h`** — the one source file without a GPL grant; a doubly-linked list was written independently for the port instead.
- **All original bitmap fonts, sprites, textures, and theme art** — unresolved or explicitly third-party provenance; replaced by new CC0-1.0 SVG art (`public/assets-clean/`) covering bubbles, cannon, HUD icons and eight theme backgrounds.
- **All original WAV/OGG audio** — two of the three checked files carry explicit, non-project third-party copyright/conversion credits; replaced by procedural Web Audio (synthesized SFX and music, rebalanced in Milestone 13).
- **SDL/SDL_image/SDL_mixer/libxml2 native rendering, audio and windowing** — replaced by Canvas 2D, Web Audio and the browser's own DOM; no native counterpart to preserve.
- **The GP2X/GBAX2006 port's 320×240 / 0.4× scale and ~30 Hz timing** — a handheld-specific tuning choice by that port, not the upstream baseline; the restoration keeps the OS4/upstream 800×600, 25 Hz values instead and uses the GP2X binary only as independent compiled corroboration.
- **Live native-runtime differential capture and a real-device/browser test matrix** — explicitly documented as pending in `RELEASE-CHECKLIST.md`; this repository ships as a Release Candidate, not a parity-certified final release, for exactly this reason.
