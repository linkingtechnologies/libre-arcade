# Project lineage and title split

The historical upstream started as **Virtual Toybox Puzzle Collection**, containing three games. Libre Arcade preserves that common origin but publishes the three games as independent titles:

- **Libre Jigsaw** — this repository. It follows the jigsaw line through the later Libre Jigsaw evolution.
- **Sliding Tile Puzzle** — separate restoration project, derived from the Virtual Toybox sliding-tile game.
- **Spinning Tile Puzzle** — separate restoration project, derived from the Virtual Toybox seven-hex spinning game.

This split is a packaging and product decision only. It does not rewrite the historical genealogy. Credits and archaeology notes should continue to identify Virtual Toybox Puzzle Collection and Jonathan Hulka as the common origin.
