## Current visual boundary

The Mosaic tile artwork and the underlying Memonix title/menu artwork are now recovered originals. The main title screen preserves the original 800×600 composition and selector geometry, but reassigns three unavailable suite mode slots to standalone navigation. Bilingual labels and the remaining secondary screens are restoration overlays because no historical standalone Mosaic shell exists.
