## Historical media

`reference/dkbk/dkbk.dat` contains historical media with mixed or insufficiently documented provenance. The full laboratory package retains it for archaeology; the public repo-safe package omits it. The public package also omits the historical PCX screenshots. These binary historical materials are never used by the browser runtime.

## Clean runtime replacements

The web runtime does not redistribute the historical Allegro audio samples. Replacement sound effects are synthesized by `public/src/audio.js` and are part of this GPL-3.0-or-later restoration. Their timbres are newly created and are not copies or transformations of the historical samples.
