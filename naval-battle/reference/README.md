## Warboats 0.51-ascii

The pristine historical archive and extracted source are stored under `reference/warboats-0.51/`. This is the primary source-port specimen used by 🐽’s Naval Battle.

## Bataille Navale OS4 (2009)

The preserved AmigaOS4 archive is stored under `reference/bataille-navale-os4-2009/`. The JavaScript player is a reconstruction from binary/debug archaeology, not a recovered source port.

Rejected candidates and licence gates are tracked in `specs/candidates.md`; their source archives are intentionally not bundled here.
