# Historical Battleship AI candidates

| Candidate | Period | AI signal | Licence | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Warboats 0.51-ascii** | 2009 | four historical difficulty levels | GPL-2.0-or-later source headers; GPLv3 text bundled | **active source port** |
| Bataille Navale OS4 | 2009 | simple hunt/target | GPLv3 in supplied archive | active binary reconstruction |
| Gnome Batalla Naval / `gbnrobot` | 2001–2002 | dedicated robot client | GPLv2-only under current evidence | **discarded for GPLv3 port** |
| Simple Battleship 0.0.1 | 2010 | unknown/likely basic | SourceForge labels GPLv3 | fallback candidate |
| AI-Battle | 2011 | framework for Battleships/minesweeper AIs | LGPLv3 | investigate later |
| BattleShipCACCPM | 2014 | dedicated AI source directory | GPLv3 | compatible but outside preferred window |

Warboats is now the primary historical AI specimen because its pristine source is available, its grant is GPLv3-compatible, and its four levels provide meaningful behavioural progression for benchmarking.

Gnome Batalla Naval is kept only as a documented rejected candidate. Its source archive, source map, adapter placeholder and alternate fleet profile are not part of the production release.
