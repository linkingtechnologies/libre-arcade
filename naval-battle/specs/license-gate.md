## Bataille Navale OS4 (2009)

The supplied archive contains the GNU GPL version 3 license text. The reconstructed AI in BattleLab is based on binary/debug archaeology and remains explicitly labelled as a reconstruction until source-level parity becomes possible.

**BattleLab status:** eligible for the GPLv3 implementation track.

## Candidate gate

Before a new historical AI enters `/src/players`, record:

- exact upstream version and date;
- original source location;
- licence text/header from the source itself, not only hosting-site metadata;
- whether the grant is GPLv3-compatible;
- AI source files/functions to be preserved;
- whether the implementation is a direct port, binary reconstruction, or independent reimplementation.
