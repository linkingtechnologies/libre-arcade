# reference/

The historical material behind this game is kept light and reproducible, not as a copy of the whole archive.

- `bomber-1.0/SOURCE.md` says where the archive comes from (SourceForge project `bomberj2me`), gives its name, size, date and SHA-256, and tells how to fetch it again and check it.
- `bomber-1.0/ARCHIVE_SHA256.txt` lists the 12 files of the archive with their SHA-256.
- `bomber-1.0/src/`, `bin/` and `CHECKSUMS.txt` keep what is needed to document and run the game: the two Java sources, the GPL text shipped with them, the descriptor and the manifest, unmodified.
- The jar, the compiled classes and the four PNG resources are not redistributed: nothing in the archive says who drew the images or under which licence. The same rule is used for XO World.

The zip is not here on purpose. Recover it from the address in `SOURCE.md`; `PROVENANCE.md` says how it was checked against the one used for this restoration (byte for byte identical).
