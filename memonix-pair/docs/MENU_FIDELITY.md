# Memonix title/menu fidelity

## Historical elements restored

- `DATA/mainmenu.jpg` is drawn as the untouched 800×600 historical viewport.
- Pair keeps its original suite slot at **(496,66)** with the recovered `DATA/pr_p.bmp` preview.
- The original `DATA/main_game.bmp` hover frame is reused after converting its black colour-key to alpha.
- The other three historical 128×128 mode windows remain in their original coordinates and are reassigned to standalone Instructions, Options and Credits.
- The two lower utility rows retain the original Memonix menu geometry.

## Standalone adaptations

The original screen selected Mosaic, Builder, Pair and Jig-Saw. In this standalone restoration only Pair launches a game. The remaining windows are navigation surfaces built from recovered Pair card artwork. Bilingual labels, language/audio controls, Top Scores and the direct New Game control are preservation-layer additions.

The copyright line embedded in `mainmenu.jpg` remains untouched because it is part of the recovered historical artwork.
