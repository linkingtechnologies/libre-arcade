The historical source package states a dual-licensing model with a GNU GPL version 3 open-source option. The exact recovered license text is preserved at `reference/Memonix-License.txt`.

This restoration distributes lossless PNG conversions of the recovered 64×64 Pair artwork: the card back plus 71 toy faces. `toys-015.bmp` is absent in the original data set and is intentionally not fabricated.

Recovered common Memonix UI artwork is also used for historical title, preview and gameplay fidelity, including `mainmenu.jpg`, `start.jpg`, `start2.jpg`, `start3.jpg`, `game.jpg`, `box.jpg`, the relevant `game2.bmp` region and `BUILDER/0.bmp`. Losslessly converted bitmap assets are documented in the included manifests.
