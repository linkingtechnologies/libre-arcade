1. Two known upper-boundary escape replays and 567 seeded bumper-driven starting states were protected; this does not prove every possible escape is eliminated or that complete Box2D trajectories match.
2. Entry into the launch lane from its upper opening and initial states embedded in a divider or outer wall still require separate checks.
3. The historical 990-state scan retained mismatched upper-curve contacts. The later containment test does not prove all such mismatches were resolved.
4. The integrated replay diverges from the original near frames 407–434. Passing isolated regressions is not whole-game native certification.
5. Direct static-host delivery and real-device testing—including three-ball play, audio, multitouch and resize—remain outstanding.
6. Establish redistribution rights for the assets embedded in the original JAR **before** adding the binary to any public Git repository, including an archaeology folder. The binary is excluded from this public-commit candidate.
