# Early boundary investigation — open issues at that stage

1. **Narrow launch lane and outer-right wall.** The ball center has about 3 mm of free horizontal space between the divider (x=.710–.720 m) and outside wall (x=.750–.760 m), after accounting for its .0135 m radius. In the initial comparison, all 15 lane-side starts and all 15 right-wall starts disagreed with the native reference; simultaneous two-wall contacts still needed analysis. See the later coupled-lane report for subsequent progress.
2. **Upper curves.** The original central-ceiling check covered x=.38 m, not the 30 thin polygon segments on either side of the top arc. Valid initial states within the curve, native reproductions and safe handling of overlapping contacts were needed.
3. **Full-game replay.** Isolated native checks passed while the integrated replay first differed by over 1 mm around frame 407 and had material velocity divergence around frame 434.
4. **Physical acceptance.** The managed Chromium gate used exact inline payloads because `file://` and localhost were blocked. Complete games on hosted Chrome/Firefox/Safari and real phones, including sound and multitouch, were still untested.

Archive integrity and isolated contact parity must not be presented as certification that every possible ball escape is eliminated. This report is historical; consult the latest open-issues document for the remaining work.
