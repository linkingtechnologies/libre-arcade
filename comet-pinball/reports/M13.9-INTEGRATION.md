# Boundary collision investigation — initial focused integration

## Baseline and scope

The initial patch was based on the verified earlier game archive (`4e23d2006a784428483f741364a8a79a5ce13f122023514a97cb775b7fea7911`). All historical files were unchanged at that stage. Geometry, friction, restitution, gravity, ball parameters, controls, score, audio, camera and visuals were not tuned.

## Focused changes

1. **Playfield → launch-lane divider:** when a step starts on the playfield side, the original `plunger-tube` divider participates in the existing polygon time-of-impact (TOI) calculation. Its dimensions are unchanged, no invisible wall is added and the legitimate route above the divider tip remains available.
2. **Straight central ceiling:** the historical `ceiling` fixture receives a polygon TOI check, separate from the upper-curve contact path. Only central vertical impacts were covered by this initial comparison.
3. **Contact lifecycle:** confirmed swept impacts are associated with the fixture in the frame's contact events without double-scoring; `reset()` clears the additional diagnostic fields.

## Why the first divider test covered one approach direction

The historical divider is **not** a one-way wall. The narrow gap between divider and outer right wall makes independent one-wall TOI handling unsafe when a ball is already inside the lane: the later wall can be crossed after an outdated sweep is reused. The initial patch therefore protected only the compared playfield-side approach. Later coupled two-wall tests are documented in `M13.11-LANE-CCD.md`.

## Evidence and historical limits

- All **35/35** focused playfield-side states matched the original JAR within 0.5 mm and 0.0001 m/s. In the preceding implementation, **22/35** crossed the divider when the native simulation remained on the playfield side.
- All **15/15** central-ceiling impacts met those tolerances; the previous build differed from native in 13/15 and crossed the ceiling at the end of the step in 11/15.
- **15** starts above the divider tip preserve a legitimate passage when no external wall collides. Some remaining differences concerned the curves or outer wall, not the divider.
- The 15 lane-side starts and 15 outer-right-wall states required a subsequent coupled-contact investigation; they were not counted as passing merely because other isolated states passed.
- Historical full-game replay remains distinct: first position error above 1 mm near frame 407, material velocity divergence near frame 434.

This report records a past diagnostic milestone, not proof that every possible later upper-wall or launch-lane escape is fixed. Do not hide failures with automatic resets or teleports.
