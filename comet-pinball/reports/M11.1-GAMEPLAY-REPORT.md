# M11.1 gameplay diagnosis — scope and evidence

## Prior M11 (unchanged inputs and physics)
- 100 pulse seeds, 3,600 frames: 91 complete, nine incomplete **at cutoff**.
- 100 reactive-hold seeds, 3,600 frames: three complete, 97 incomplete **at cutoff**.
- Incomplete does not mean immobile and 60 seconds is not a universal maximum pinball-ball duration.

## Follow-up — actual physics, actual drains, no artificial ball endings

| Protocol | Completed after 60s | Completed after 120s | Incomplete after 120s |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 100 pulses, unchanged input policy throughout | 91 | 94 | 6 |
| *Selected* 20 reactive scripts (seeds 0–18 and 34), pulse input only after frame 3,600 | 0 | 18 | 2 |

The six incomplete pulse-policy runs still had >2 seconds of physically moving ball during their second minute; all six had >3,500 frames of active movement. In the reactive-to-pulse subset, seed 3 still moved actively throughout the second minute; seed 34 had zero travel after it reached the upper-left resting pocket. The protocols intentionally differ: **the 20-script subset must not be extrapolated to all players, all reactive inputs, or the native game**.

## UX decision

Do not auto-end a ball after a delay or force-boost it out of the pocket. Display an optional recovery decision only after five simulated seconds of proven top-left immobility; first click shows a confirmation, cancelling preserves the ball. A user can also continue or end a ball using the existing Pause menu anywhere on the table.

## Remaining gate

A browser smoke with Chromium's inlined *exact production payload* is a rendering/control check, not a physical smartphone acceptance test. Release remains unqualified for 1.0 until manual player trials and audiovisual polish. Native full-game bitwise parity remains an independent diagnostic effort.
