# Coupled launch-lane wall handling

## Failure mechanism

The launch corridor is 30 mm wide while the ball is 27 mm in diameter. Multiple impacts with the divider and outer-right wall can occur in one frame. Previously, an isolated TOI rebound against one wall was followed by a sweep computed **before** that rebound. That could cross the other wall or register a false impact against a distant fixture.

## Focused correction

When the ball center begins inside the lower corridor (x=.732–.738 m and .055 < y < 1.065 m) with significant horizontal velocity, the step follows a time-ordered continuous-collision sequence against the two **historical fixtures**. No invisible barriers, forced repositioning or handwritten bounce rules are introduced. Shorter sweep segments prevent fast trajectories from completely crossing a polygon before collision detection. After each impact the regular reconstructed polygon solver applies impulses, and the remaining trajectory is recalculated from the post-impact state.

When the lane solver consumes the frame, later fixtures do not replay a stale pre-impact sweep. Such replay once generated a false slingshot contact near x=.28 m even though the ball remained inside the corridor. The legitimate passage above the divider tip and the earlier playfield-side divider correction remain unchanged. Geometry, bumper forces, flippers, visuals, controls, audio, camera and scoring are untouched.

## Regression evidence and limitations

- **20** physically valid native one-step starts matched within **0.0000731 m** position and **0.0000248 m/s** velocity. Ten earlier comparison states began **inside a wall** and were not counted toward this focused parity result.
- **14** sequences of 120 frames with 1–40 m/s initial speeds in both horizontal directions produced no escape within the tested corridor interval. Up to eight wall contacts occurred in a frame without exhausting the TOI budget.
- The original 34-test suite and managed Chromium gate passed at that stage; browser checks used inline bytes when local navigation was blocked.

The result covers only the specified initial states. Entry through the top lane opening, starts already embedded in walls, remaining upper-curve cases and integrated native replay are separate tasks. Historical JAR redistribution must be cleared independently.
