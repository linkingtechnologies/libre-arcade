# Comet Pinball

Three balls, two flippers and a space-themed table. Hit the targets and try to beat your high score.

## Play

Extract the archive and open `index.html` in a modern browser. No internet connection is needed. If your browser restricts opening local pages, place the files on a static website.

**Language:** English by default; Italian is selected automatically when Italian appears among your browser language preferences. Select **EN / IT** in the top bar to switch at any time. Your explicit choice is saved on this device when the browser permits local storage.

**Desktop:** ← / A / Z / Tab controls the left flipper; → / D / L / M / Enter controls the right flipper; Space / ↓ launches; P / Esc pauses; V changes the camera.

**Phone:** use the buttons below the table. Both flippers support simultaneous touch. The camera follows the ball by default and can be changed at any time.

After the first and second balls, the next ball is prepared automatically; you launch it yourself. After the third ball you can save your high score. If a ball gets stuck, you may choose to end the turn through the pause menu.

Choose **Credits** from the main menu to see the original authors, music attribution and the [Libre Arcade project](https://linkingtechnologies.github.io/libre-arcade/).
