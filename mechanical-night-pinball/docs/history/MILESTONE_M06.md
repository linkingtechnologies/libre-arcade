# M0.6

Goal: art polish pass on the clean-room presentation while keeping the calibrated M0.4+ gameplay intact.

Highlights:
- richer playfield composition and insert art;
- improved cabinet/bezel appearance;
- clearer lane identity and lower-apron readability;
- polished active ramp overlay;
- no use of quarantined historical graphics or audio.

Non-goals:
- no gameplay rebalance;
- no parity-rule changes;
- no dependence on historical Flash/DocDonkeys art.
