How to play

Drop a disc into a column. Align four horizontally, vertically or diagonally before your opponent.

Each level draws one of the similarly skilled intelligences. The exact opponent remains hidden during normal play.

About difficulty levels

Each level includes several opponents of similar skill. A different one is chosen for every game.

Advanced

Explore individual opponents or compare them in automatic tournaments.
