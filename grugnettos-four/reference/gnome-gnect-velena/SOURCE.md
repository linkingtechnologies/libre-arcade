The retained C headers state that the Velena code was modified for inclusion
with Gnect and released under the GNU GPL with Giuliano Bertoletti's approval.
The package-level `COPYING` and SHA-256 manifest are preserved here.

The separate upstream `veleng10.zip` is intentionally **not** redistributed:
its historical custom license prohibits charging a fee and is not GPLv3
compatible. ConnectLab relies only on the explicitly GPL-released GNOME form.
