# Project history

Grugnetto's Four began as ConnectLab, a preservation and comparison framework for historical Four-in-a-Row computer players. The work progressed from a common game and player contract to source-faithful adapters, parity tests, a deterministic Arena, strength calibration and finally a player-facing Libre Arcade game.

The public release contains 23 participants grouped into six measured difficulty levels. Normal play deliberately hides the selected implementation and chooses a different opponent from the chosen level. The Advanced area retains direct Laboratory and Arena access for research and reproducibility.

Detailed provenance, licenses and preserved source snapshots remain in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`, `docs/development/PORTING_NOTES*.md` and `reference/`.
