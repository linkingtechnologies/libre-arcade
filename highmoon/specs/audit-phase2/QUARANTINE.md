# Asset quarantine

Original assets are preserved verbatim under `reference/HighMoon`, but preservation does not imply clearance for reuse in a new release.

- Code: **CLEARED** as GPL-2.0-or-later from explicit source headers.
- Planet/moon imagery: **QUARANTINE — high priority provenance trace**. No per-file attribution exists in the archive.
- UFO/HUD/projectile/explosion/font graphics: **QUARANTINE** pending authorship/source confirmation. The project-wide GPL statement is evidence of upstream intent, but not proof that every incorporated third-party asset was relicensable.
- Audio: **QUARANTINE**. Multiple samples demonstrably share lineage with the historic OpenOffice/LibreOffice sound gallery; exact historical source/license must be mapped per file before reuse.
- Music: none found in 1.2.4.

Safest Libre Arcade route if provenance cannot be closed: retain original bytes only in the archaeological `/reference` area where legally appropriate, and use newly-created or independently-cleared replacement assets in the playable restoration.
