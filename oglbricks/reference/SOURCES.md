## Licensing boundary

The OGLBricks source code carries the MIT license preserved in `OGLBricks-MIT-LICENSE.txt`. The HTML5 port is distributed under GPLv3-or-later while retaining the original copyright and MIT notice for source-derived material.

The historical Windows bundle includes third-party components such as Qt/Assimp runtime files. Their redistribution terms are separate from the OGLBricks source license, which is why the original Windows archive and DLLs are not copied into this public repository package.

No artwork, audio or other material from any commercial falling-block game is included in the Libre Arcade port. It carries the piece geometry derived from the MIT-licensed OGLBricks source and nothing else: the board, the pieces and the sound effects are drawn and synthesized by the port itself.
