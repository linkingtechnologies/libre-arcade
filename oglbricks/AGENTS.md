# AGENTS.md

- License: the original is MIT (Alexey Markarov, notice in `reference/OGLBricks-MIT-LICENSE.txt`), the port is GPL-3.0-or-later. The MIT attribution travels with the shape catalog specifically: keep it in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` and do not let the GPL notice absorb it.
- `public/` **is** the game: `public/index.html` at the root, `public/js/` beside it, no `src/` copy and nothing to synchronize. The delivered package kept two byte-identical trees and a script to copy one onto the other; that is gone and must not come back.
- `specs/original-shapes.json` and `specs/original-spatial-contract.json` are the extraction of the original `Shape.cpp`. They are evidence, not output: never regenerate them from `public/js/shapes.js`, which would make `test/site.test.mjs` compare the port with itself. If a shape ever has to change, change the extraction from the original `Shape.cpp` first. That file is not in this repository: `reference/SOURCES.md` says where to fetch the archive and `reference/MANIFEST.sha256` what it must hash to.
- The score formula is `clearedLines² × fieldWidth × speed`, from the original C++. One native observation reported 10 points where it gives 40 (`specs/AUDIT.md`, O08). Do not "fix" the formula to match that number, and do not drop the discrepancy from the documentation; it is the port's one open parity question.
- Keep the original's quirks: the next piece type excludes the current one, spawn searches centre then right then left, and a rotation that does not fit is refused with no wall kick. The single documented departure is that one enabled category repeats its shape instead of reproducing the original's `InvalidType` path.
- Adaptations that must never be described as original behavior: xorshift32 in place of `qrand()`, the 300 ms lock delay, Canvas 2D instead of OpenGL, the JSON save format, English and Italian, touch controls and the synthesized audio cues. The original `.sg` and `settings.bin` are deliberately not read.
- `test/site.test.mjs` lists every remote script in the page and allows exactly the GoatCounter beacon. Narrow that list if analytics change; never delete the assertion.
- `specs/` holds four files and should stay that size: `AUDIT.md` (the archaeology and the six milestones, condensed from the 19 delivered QA files), `port-map.md` and the two extractions. Do not reintroduce per-milestone documents or commit raw test-runner output.
- No frameworks, bundler or backend. `npm run check` (ESLint plus 38 Node tests) must pass before packaging, and `python3 tools/smoke_http.py` checks the served tree.
