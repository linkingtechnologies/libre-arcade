## Runtime assets

Graphics, bitmap fonts, level-result imagery, WAV effects and tracker music originate in the Njam distribution. Debian's copyright metadata classifies the upstream package `Files: *` as GPL-2.0+.

The web runtime uses browser-friendly PNG copies of the original BMP sprite/skin/font resources where applicable. The original tracker files `satisfy.xm`, `ritam.s3m` and `dali.xm` are included unchanged and are accompanied by Ogg Vorbis playback transcodes generated solely for browser compatibility.

The untouched historical archives and license texts remain under `reference/archives/` for auditability.

The web-specific source (`src/`, `index.html`, `style.css`, tests and specifications) is licensed GPL-3.0-or-later.
