## Historical textures — not included

The upstream 0.9 archive contained `menu.bmp`, `background.bmp`, and `spiral.bmp`. Their provenance is documented in the upstream README. Because sufficient GPL-compatible redistribution evidence was not found for the externally sourced media, these files are excluded from this repository and from the web build.

## Replacement artwork included in this restoration

The browser port includes three newly created replacement images under `assets/`: `game-background.png`, `floor-vortex.png`, and `menu-background.png`. They were created specifically for this restoration in September 2026 and are not the historical PSY PONG 3D bitmap files. They are distributed as part of this project under GPL-3.0-or-later. See `assets/ASSET_PROVENANCE.md`.
