# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release in Libre Arcade: Turkey Cooking Simulator (Game Off 2013), ported from the restoration package `turkey-cooking-simulator-complete-rc1.zip` and moved to the original's last commit of 1 December 2013 (`13afa12`), whose thermal model is checked tick for tick against the original's own `model.js`. Temperatures can be shown in °F or °C, and the sounds and tunes are the original's own (public domain or CC0). See `PROVENANCE.md` and `specs/port-map.md`.
