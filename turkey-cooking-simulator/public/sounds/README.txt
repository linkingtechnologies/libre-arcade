Sounds of the original Turkey Cooking Simulator
================================================

These twelve MP3 files are the original game's own sound files, byte for byte, taken from commit 13afa1230761fa24d13d9df4324ccafb504398bc
(1 December 2013) of https://github.com/fernjager/game-off-2013 and renamed. They are the ones that the original's js/soundmanager.js
registers and plays (the clicks, the buzz for an error, the purchase, the ding, the oven alarm's double beep, the shop entrance, the four oven-door
sounds, and the two tunes). Nothing was re-encoded or edited.

Licence. The original's res/sound/Audio Sources.txt says: "Unless Otherwise noted, all sounds and music are in the public Domain." Its credits screen
lists, as the sources of its music and sounds:

  Turkey in the Straw   American folk song, performed by the United States Air Force Band   Public Domain
  Waterford             Kevin MacLeod, FreePD.com                                           Public Domain

The credits do not say which file is which effect, and the oven-door and shop-entrance files carry no credit of their own, so they are covered
by the sources file's general statement and by nothing more specific. Everything here is public domain or CC0 as far as the original says;
that is the original's statement, not something that was independently established.
