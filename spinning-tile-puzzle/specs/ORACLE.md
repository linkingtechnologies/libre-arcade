# Behavioral oracle

1. A new game shows the selected photograph as the full background with a central 2–3–2 cluster of seven hexagonal tiles.
2. Six interaction vertices lie between groups of three tiles.
3. Clockwise spin changes only the three tiles at that vertex; counter-clockwise is its inverse.
4. Three clockwise spins at the same vertex restore those three positions and orientations.
5. Mix performs exactly five passes of the historical `SpinnerHandler.mix()` algorithm.
6. The puzzle is solved only when every location contains its original tile with rotation step 0.
7. A click/tap after solving mixes again.
8. Gallery, preview, custom image, background color, IT/EN and Credits must work without network access.
