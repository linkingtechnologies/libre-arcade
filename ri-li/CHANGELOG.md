# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release in Libre Arcade, promoted from the restoration package `rili-m18.zip` after a comparison with the original program, compiled and run next to the page on a virtual clock, and a check in a real browser.

- An arcade game for one player: the 50 levels, the three difficulties, the quiz after each level, the table of scores with its name entry, the menus and the sound effects of Ri-li 2.0.1 (C++/SDL, 2007) by Dominique Roux-Serret, with music by Maf464
- Recorded where the original comes from (`reference/`): the source release, untouched, with its SHA-256, size and what is known about its origin (the size agrees with SourceForge's listing; no checksum to compare was found)
- Added an executable oracle (`tools/oracle/`, `tools/*.mjs`): the original's own C++ compiled with g++ (three pointer tests that C++ no longer accepts are the only change) and linked against SDL 1.2.15 built from the official archive, run headless on a virtual clock with scripted keys, and compared with the page's own code on the same keys: the state of the train and the board, the random numbers, the sounds, the list of what was drawn and the pictures
- Added the other 17 languages of the original (German, Spanish, French, Arabic, Chinese, Japanese, Russian, Slovak, Breton, Portuguese, Swedish, Esperanto, Hungarian, Turkish, Polish, Dutch and Korean), with the original's own pictures: 1,343 label pictures and 17 labels of the languages' names decoded without loss from the original's `language.XX` files (`tools/extract-languages.mjs`), checked pixel for pixel with them (`tools/check-assets.mjs`: 1,501 pictures in 19 languages), loaded when a language is chosen; the language menu is the original's, with its 19 labels, and a button in the bar shows the language's code and opens it from any screen. The page's own texts (bar, guide, credits, status line) are in Italian and English, and in English with the other 17
- Fixed what the comparison showed to differ from the original, in the package's page:
  - the held key (the original reads it in every frame; the page read it once) and the arrow on the board, which follows the player's direction while an arrow key is down, and does not exist before the first step;
  - the order of the four directions and the random numbers, which the original carries from one level to the next and from one game to the next, and the page began again;
  - the end of the pause after a death or a clear and of the quiz's result, which the original tests after the frame is shown (a frame later than the page did);
  - the arithmetic of the position of a point, of a wagon's angle and of the cable, which is single precision in the original and put a sprite a pixel off in the page;
  - the table of scores, the options screen and the language menu, which were the package's own drawings and are the original's now, with its numbers;
  - the pieces around the panel of the quiz, whose frame and offset take the two random numbers of a call in the order g++ computes them (the last argument first), found by comparing the picture of the quiz;
  - Escape: in a game it opens the menu of the game (Continue, Options, Exit game, with the original's background after Options), in the quiz it goes on without an answer, in the main menu it leaves;
  - the first key of a visit, which waited for the music to start (a second on a slow connection, for good in headless Chrome)
- Changed the page where the way it is put together here called for it: the original's `levels.dat` moved from `reference/` into `public/data/` so that `public/` is complete by itself, an empty icon link, the collection's visit counter before `</body>`, and the guide, which mentions Escape
- Added tests: the page against a recording of the original (`test/oracle.test.mjs`, which needs neither WSL nor SDL), the source archive and the data (`test/assets.test.mjs`, `test/reference.test.mjs`, with the pictures compared with the original's data files when `RILI_DATA` is set) and the package's own 12 test scripts, run unmodified against `public/` in a temporary copy of its layout, eight of which pass and four of which are expected to fail, with the reasons (`test/package.test.mjs`)
- Checked in a real browser (Chromium-based): the menus, the options with their volumes, the language menu, a game, Escape and the menu of the game, the click on a button, the table of scores
- Joined the Libre Arcade collection: the page at the root of `public/`, `npm test` runs `node --test`, and the collection scaffolding was added (`scripts/serve.mjs`, `scripts/build.mjs`, an ESLint config, `.gitignore`, `.gitattributes`, the GoatCounter snippet, the "Play here" link, the provenance, the port map, the notices, the story, five screenshots)
- Kept the package's own documents, unmodified, in `docs/development/package/`, with a note on what they are and a list of what was changed in its JavaScript (`docs/development/CHANGES.md`)

`npm test` passes 32 tests, and a 33rd (the pictures against the original's data files) when `RILI_DATA` is set; `node tools/oracle.mjs check` plays 49 scenarios (89,643 frames), `check route --all` the route of each of the 50 levels (122,275 frames), `menus` 35 scenarios (150 pictures, among them the language menu, a choice of language and a tour of the screens in each of the 18 languages other than English) and `quiz-pictures` the quiz in three languages, all against the original: no difference in the state, the random numbers, the sounds, the list of what was drawn or the pictures.

**Known limits.** The page's own texts are in Italian and English only (English with the other 17 languages), and the original's hidden level editor (`tide` in the main menu) is not ported. The music is the package's conversion of the tracker files, not compared with the originals. The original's pointer is left out of the comparison, the places that answer a click are the page's own, and a frame longer than 16 ms is not compared (the original's step follows it). The pause after a death or a clear, if Escape is pressed in it, waits in the page and goes on behind the menu in the original. What the page adds (the touch pad, the buttons, the guide, the credits, the reset, the browser's full screen) is not compared with the original. See [`specs/port-map.md`](specs/port-map.md) and [`PROVENANCE.md`](PROVENANCE.md).
