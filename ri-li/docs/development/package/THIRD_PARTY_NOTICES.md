## Original music

`menu.mod`, `jeu1.xm` and `jeu2.xm` are preserved under `reference/audio-original/`. The upstream `COPYING.Music` explicitly identifies Maf464 / Charcosset B. and includes GNU GPL v3 terms. The `.ogg` files in `/audio` are browser-playable derivatives of those tracker originals.

## Sound effects

The nine WAV effects in `/audio` are byte-identical to the files in the original Ri-li 2.0.1 source release and to the copies preserved in `reference/audio-original/`. The upstream archive does not provide a separate per-effect author attribution in the notices located during the audit; they are therefore retained with that provenance note rather than assigned an invented author.
