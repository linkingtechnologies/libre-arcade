# Grugnetto's Board — Four Worlds design boundary

The playable restoration must not be a cosmetic reskin of the recovered 40-space regional boards. The frozen v1 board rebuilds the experience around the four worlds present in the supplied current Grugnetto Go! sources.

## Structural choices

- 32 spaces total;
- detention at index 13 rather than a corner;
- move-to-detention at index 30;
- four site groups, one per Grugnetto Go! world;
- exactly four purchasable sites per world (16 sites total);
- three Portals at indices 5, 16 and 26 (internally still `hub` for KludgopolB AI parity);
- two Special Places at indices 12 and 24 (internally `service`);
- four event spaces at indices 2, 10, 18 and 28;
- two cost/tax spaces at indices 4 and 22;
- two neutral/rest spaces at indices 8 and 20;
- new event texts/effects and original Grugnetto terminology.

## World groups

1. Prato di Casa / Home Meadow — indices 1, 3, 6, 7
2. Bosco degli Scoiattoli / Squirrel Forest — indices 9, 11, 14, 15
3. Dune Dorate / Golden Dunes — indices 17, 19, 21, 23
4. Miniera di Pietra / Stone Mine — indices 25, 27, 29, 31

The v1 board is now frozen. Any future balance change must create a new content version; the historical parity target is the engine/AI behaviour, not the original UK/US board data.
