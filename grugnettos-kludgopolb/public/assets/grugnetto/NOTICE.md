# Grugnetto original art

These assets were copied from the user-supplied `grugnetto-go.zip` for use in **Grugnetto's KludgopolB**:

- `grugnetto_idle.png`
- `collectable_coin.png`
- `collectable_acorn.png`
- `collectable_key.png`
- `flags/flag_green_a.png`, `flags/flag_blue_a.png`, `flags/flag_red_a.png` (added in board package v1.4, as the three Portal icons — the same goal-flag art used per world in Grugnetto Go itself)

They are original Grugnetto artwork and remain:

> Copyright (c) 2026 Umberto Bresciani. All rights reserved.

They are not relicensed under GPL-3.0 by this repository. The user explicitly requested their reuse in this Grugnetto project. The GPL applies to the game code, not to these artwork files.
- `grugnetto_token.svg`: project-specific flat vector pawn (a pig's snout, outlined like the CPU tokens) derived from Grugnetto's visual identity for board readability.

## World icons

`world-icons/home-meadow.png`, `golden-dunes.png` and `stone-mine.png` are cropped, centred and resized copies of the Kenney grass, sand and stone terrain blocks from Kenney's New Platformer Pack (CC0 1.0, see `../third_party/kenney/License.txt`); `squirrel-woods.png` is a resized copy of `collectable_acorn.png` above (Grugnetto original art, all rights reserved). They replaced earlier opaque icons of undocumented provenance.
