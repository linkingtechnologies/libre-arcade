## Grugnetto project assets

The SVG artwork under `public/assets/icons/` was created specifically for Grugnetto’s Goose. Runtime sound effects are project-authored. Special cues are synthesized by project code with the Web Audio API; dice, pawn-step, unlock and victory fallbacks are generated PCM WAV files under `public/assets/audio/`.
