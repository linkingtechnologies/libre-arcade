# Quarantined historical originals

The following locally examined originals are intentionally **not** included in the production repository. Their identities/hashes are preserved in `manifest.json`.

- `game-of-the-goose-main.zip` — code/package metadata indicates ISC, but bundled SVG provenance is unresolved.
- `SnakesLadders.zip` — SourceForge metadata says GPLv3, while the archive itself lacks LICENSE/COPYING and media provenance is unresolved.
- `LudoXv22-Source+Executable.rar` and `LudoXv21-Source+Executable.rar` — SourceForge labels GPLv2, but the archive does not resolve GPLv2-only vs GPLv2-or-later and asset provenance is unresolved.
- `glparchis-20181125.tar.gz` — package/code has strong GPLv3 evidence, but the complete asset provenance ledger is not closed.
- `goose-game` pinned snapshot — repository metadata contains a GPLv3/ISC conflict.
- Tibetan Sho exact source snapshot — direct source/asset audit remains incomplete.

This is a distribution decision, not a claim that the projects are non-free. It prevents the production package from converting incomplete evidence into an unsupported redistribution claim.
