## What was and was not ported

Ported: `Tilemap` (obstacle placement, free-tile search, carrot spawning, nearest free carrot), `Player`
(movement, pulling, push, totem, carrot count), `Enemy` (spawn, targeting, A* path following, pulling, push
reaction, totem reaction) and the `main` scene's loop (timer, game over, difficulty turns, spawn ticks).

Not ported: the jQuery UI dialogs, splash screen and menu, the leaderboards (Playtomic, Clay.io, Game
Jolt, Facebook), the debug FPS counter, the Safari audio workaround, Crafty's rendering and the
`MagicPush`/`MagicFork` entities as engine objects (their effects are drawn by `src/render.js`).

## Licence evidence

Level of evidence for each licensing claim, strongest first:

- **Read in the artefact itself:** the MIT text and header of every original source file; `art/LICENSE`
  ("All images are (c) Stremena Tuzsuzova"); the original README's sentence that the MIT licence covers the
  game without its art; the artist's website footer ("All Rights Reserved"), read 22 Nov 2026.
- **Read on the authoritative page, 22 Nov 2026:** the OpenGameArt page for the music (licences OGA-BY 3.0
  and CC0); the Freesound pages of the nine credited clips (current licences, which may differ from
  those in force in 2012); Marijn Haverbeke's page for the heap (CC BY 3.0).
- **Not established:** which Freesound clip each sound-effect file was made from, and so the licence of
  each file. This is why none is used.
- **Not established:** the exact copyright year and holder line of javascript-astar's licence (the file
  headers say only "Freely distributable under the MIT License").
