Help

Select a cell, then enter a digit. Pencil mode adds candidate marks. Undo, redo, hint and conflict checking follow the original 5ud0ku behaviour.

Custom Puzzle lets you enter any Sudoku. “Solve” displays the first solution; “Solve & Play” keeps your givens and starts a playable game. As in the original, a puzzle may have more than one solution.

Keyboard: arrows move, 1–9 enter a digit, Delete clears, P toggles Pencil, Ctrl/Cmd+Z undoes and Ctrl/Cmd+Y redoes.
