# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release in Libre Arcade, promoted from the restoration package 1.0.0 (its milestone M11) after a check in a real browser and against the original Java code.

- Sudoku with the generator, the two solvers, the undo history and the save layout of 5ud0ku 1.8 (Ive&Case, 2006), ported from the Java ME source. Three levels and puzzles of your own, pencil marks, hints, a check for clashes, named saves and automatic saving, English and Italian. The puzzle work runs in a Web Worker. No original image is redistributed.
- Preserved the original source under `reference/5ud0ku-1.8/`: the 46 text files of the source archive and the jar's descriptor, unmodified, with a checksum list, a manifest of all 55 files of the archive, and a record of where the archive and the jar come from (`SOURCE.md`). The archive, the jar and the nine images are not kept (`reference/README.md`). The preserved files are byte-exact (`.gitattributes`).
- Added an executable oracle: ten original classes compiled unmodified with three phone-only stand-ins and driven by `tools/java-oracle.mjs`. 83,744 command lines (500 Easy, 500 Normal and 300 Hard games run through the original `CreatorTask`, 514 solver puzzles, 600 sequences of moves, 10 random-number streams, a save written by the original classes) give identical answers in the port, on Java 8 and on Java 21. The package's three generated games and ten solver puzzles are the original's own output. A recorded run replays in `npm test` without Java; `npm run test:live` runs the original again. Twenty-six deliberate faults put into the port one at a time were caught 23 times (see `specs/port-map.md`).
- Added four behaviours of the original phone interface that the package had left out (read from `SudokuUI`, `SudokuStateControl` and `SudokuMidlet`, see `specs/port-map.md`): entering the digit that is already in a cell clears it, and a cell that shows a digit takes pen mode even when pencil mode is on; **Show solution** (the answer in every cell, your own digits marked right or wrong with a tick or a cross, input disabled, back with the menu or Esc); **Reset game** (digits, notes, time and history cleared, timer restarted; the browser version asks first); **Edit this puzzle** (the given numbers of the game on the board become an editable puzzle, on a copy, with Cancel returning to the game). New tests in `test/m12-original-features.test.js`; the menu in two columns on short landscape screens.
- Fixed the finishing rule: a board now counts as finished when it is full and valid, as in the original, instead of when it equals the stored answer. The two agree for generated puzzles; for a puzzle you entered with several solutions, any valid completion now wins. Checked in a browser.
- Fixed the tool buttons on screens up to 380 pixels wide: their labels were hidden with `display:none`, which also removed them from the accessible names; they are now visually hidden and still read out.
- Fixed the layout of short landscape screens. At 568 by 320 pixels the board was 94 pixels wide because the side-by-side layout began at 650 pixels; it now begins at 400 pixels wide (for screens up to 500 pixels high and at least 1.25 times as wide as high) and gives a 244-pixel board there. In that layout the tool buttons are icons only, in both languages: in Italian the six labels did not fit the 210-pixel column and the Hint and Check buttons left the screen.
- Windows between 501 and 620 pixels high and wider than 720 pixels now use the full layout, with the controls beside the board, instead of the compact one with the controls below it (a 1024 by 501 window went from a 275-pixel board to 403).
- The credits say "HTML5 port" instead of "HTML5 restoration and preservation", and "Versione HTML5" in Italian.
- Checked in a real browser: no vertical or horizontal scroll and no control outside the window at 35 window sizes in English (320 by 568 to 2560 by 1440, and landscape from 400 by 300), and at a dozen in Italian. Playing with mouse and keyboard (digits, pencil marks, arrows, Delete, undo and redo, hint, the digit entered twice, pencil mode on a cell with a digit), the solution view in both languages and on a phone, reset with and without confirmation, editing the current puzzle with Cancel and with Solve & Play, a game won with the dialog, saving, loading and deleting, custom puzzles (unique, multiple, invalid, cancelled with Escape), the beginner and clash settings, erasing local data, restoring after a reload, Easy, Normal and Hard generation in the worker. No console error and no failed request.
- Joined the Libre Arcade collection: the browser files in `public/` (the page at the root of `public/`), `npm test` runs `node --test`, and the collection scaffolding was added (`scripts/serve.mjs`, `scripts/build.mjs`, an ESLint config, `.gitignore`, `.gitattributes`, the GoatCounter snippet, the "Play here" link, the provenance, archaeology and port-map documents, the story). The delivered tests moved to `public/src/...` paths; two unused imports and variables were removed to satisfy the linter, and the production test now checks the preserved original instead of the archive it no longer holds.
- Made the worker test tolerate a loaded machine: the 17-given Hard fixture, which takes about 5 seconds on a quiet machine, took 16 on a busy one and exceeded the 15-second limit of the test, which is now 120 seconds.
- Kept the package's development notes, captured logs and screenshots, with their milestone labels, unmodified in `docs/development/`.

**Known limits.** A window narrower than 400 pixels and lower than about 330 pixels (for example 399 by 300) shows a board under 170 pixels wide; no common device has that shape. The differences from the original's user interface that were found by reading it and not changed (the marks on the number pad, the pencil-mark menu commands, the colour preferences, the quick-save key, the pointer mode, the other languages) are in `specs/port-map.md`.
